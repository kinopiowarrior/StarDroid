# StarDroid 1.9.12

- Classic building placement uses a lighter tint. Remastered ground orders use the installed animated waypoint. The resource HUD fits mixed-race supply counters inside the usable screen area.
- Story transitions keep the previous image visible until the next clip supplies a frame.
- Research and upgrade announcements follow the race of the building. Resource and energy messages use the unit that issued the command.
- Updated Brazilian Portuguese language detection and campaign voice selection, including installed Remastered speech files.
- SCVs report a shortage of minerals or gas when they cannot pay for a repair.
- Original skirmish AI can send an idle surplus force. Nearby campaign defenders respond locally when enabled. Blocked troops, sieged tanks and burrowed Lurkers can clear a passage.
- Hard AI groups Battlecruisers and improves nuclear strike, Psionic Storm and detector use.
- Nearby multiplayer names players delaying a turn and supplies connection-stage, route and ping information. All peers need the same simulation build.
- Added command-queue and control-group regression tests using sc4j and sc4j-scr references. See THIRD_PARTY.md.
