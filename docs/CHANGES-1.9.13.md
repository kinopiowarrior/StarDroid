# StarDroid 1.9.13

- An SCV that runs out of minerals or gas during a repair stops quietly. Both touch commands can repair Duke’s ship on Norad II after Raynor rescues the base.
- Text and speech choices stay independent across all 13 supported locale tags. The import checks recognize regional tags, legacy table encodings and separate Classic and Remastered language assets.
- Stock campaigns use installed speech in the selected language. Custom campaign recordings keep their map-defined audio.
- A Hatchery with a valid spawn site now waits the normal cycle when the game has exhausted its unit slots. Blocked terrain still retries sooner.
- Dying tanks ignore siege and unsiege commands. Dying cloaking units also ignore cloak and decloak commands.
- Lurker spines hit a friendly unit when it is explicitly targeted. Other nearby friendly units keep their normal protection.
- Nearby matches require build 194 on every device. The save format remains at schema 28.
